# Food & Meals

## Communal Meals
Breakfast is self-serve, 8-10am. Bread, eggs, fruit, and coffee are stocked.

Lunch is DIY — people usually coordinate in the group chat around noon.

Dinner is communal on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 7pm. Two people cook each time — sign up on the kitchen whiteboard. Budget is €15 per meal for the whole group.

## Dietary Notes
Let the group know your dietary needs so cooks can plan. So far we know:
- Alice is vegan and allergic to nuts
- Marco is gluten-free

## Local Food
The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, 10 minute walk down the hill. Turn left out of the house, follow the road downhill.

There's a good bakery called La Masa on Calle Mayor — opens at 7am.

For dinner out, Taberna El Rincón does great tapas. About 15 minutes walk toward the old town.

## Kitchen Rules
Wash your dishes after cooking. Don't leave food unlabeled in the fridge — anything unlabeled for more than 2 days gets composted.
