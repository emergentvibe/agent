# Norms

## Shared Spaces
Clean up after yourself in the kitchen. If you use the last of something (coffee, milk, eggs), add it to the shopping list on the fridge.

Shoes off inside the house.

## Communication
The Telegram group is the main communication channel. If something is urgent, message directly. If it can wait, post in the group.

Don't assume silence means agreement. If you want to know where people stand on something, ask explicitly.

## Noise
Quiet hours are 10pm to 8am. Music in shared spaces needs group consent. The studio is soundproofed — go wild in there.

## Guests
Guests are welcome during the day. Overnight guests need a heads-up in the group chat at least 24 hours in advance. If anyone objects, find a compromise.

## Decisions
For day-to-day stuff (what's for dinner, when to do a group activity): just coordinate in the chat.

For things that affect everyone (changing quiet hours, adding a new housemate, spending shared money): bring it up in the group chat and make sure everyone who cares has a chance to weigh in before acting.
