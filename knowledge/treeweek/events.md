# Events and the daily rhythm

## The shape of a normal day
Every day runs on the same spine:

- **09:00** — Breakfast
- **09:30** — Team meeting
- **10:00** — Volunteer meeting
- **13:00** — Lunch
- **18:00** — Dinner

Everything else is self-organised. Workshops, jams and activities emerge from whoever wants to
run them — there is no fixed programme, and that is deliberate.

## Kitchen shift blocks
Five blocks a day, the same times every day. Your shifts are on the printed rota and in the bot
(`/myrota`).

- **10:30–13:00** — Lunch Cooks (2.5h)
- **11:30–12:30** — Dish Team (1h)
- **14:00–18:00** — Dinner Cooks (4h)
- **15:00–17:00** — Dish Team (2h)
- **19:00–21:00** — Dish Team (2h)

Note that the 11:30 dish shift sits inside the lunch block, and the 15:00 dish shift sits inside
the dinner block. That overlap is intentional.

## Tuesday 22 September — day 1, arrivals
People arrive through the afternoon on the shuttle runs. **19:00 opening speech. 20:00 PARTY.**

Day 1 kitchen shifts are covered by crew and volunteers, because most people are still on a train.

## Wednesday 23 September — day 2
Normal rhythm.

## Thursday 24 September — day 3
Normal rhythm.

## Friday 25 September — day 4
**14:00 WHIZBANG** — full house, all hands cleaning. **20:00 Talent Show**, followed by a party.

The talent show is the one to bring something to. Sing your song — that is the whole theme of
the year.

## Saturday 26 September — day 5
**12:00 mini cleanup. 14:00 possible tree planting.** This is a deliberately slow kitchen day —
dinner is light and largely leftovers, so the kitchen gets a break.

## Sunday 27 September — day 6
**20:00 PARTY** — the birthday party. Simon's birthday is Monday the 28th, so this one rolls
over midnight into it.

## Monday 28 September — day 7
**14:00 WHIZBANG** — full house, all hands. Simon's birthday. Last full day.

## Tuesday 29 September — day 8, departures
**08:00 wake up. 09:00 breakfast. 10:00 the bus leaves** for Gesundbrunnen. No kitchen shifts —
the rota covers days 1 to 7 only.

## WHIZBANG
**WHIZBANG is a full-house, all-hands clean.** Everybody stops what they're doing and the whole
house gets sorted out together. It happens twice — **Friday 25th and Monday 28th, both at 14:00.**

It is not the same thing as the mini cleanup on Saturday, which is small. WHIZBANG is everyone.

## The tree planting
There is a tree planting ceremony. It is the thing the whole event is named after.

## Things that have happened at previous TREEWEEKs
Smelling workshops. Musical circling — a non-verbal jam Emergent runs. Playfighting. Circling.
Spontaneous singing. Forest walks. Massage and bodywork. DJ sets. Hammock time. Deep
conversations about philosophy and religion that run past any reasonable hour.

None of this is scheduled. It happens because someone decides to make it happen. That someone
can be you.
