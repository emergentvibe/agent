# Flavour

## What TREEWEEK is
A small, slow, self-organised week in the German countryside for people from TPOT — the corner
of Twitter where post-rationalists, philosophers and friendly ambitious nerds found each other.
It sits in the lineage of Vibecamp, Jesscamp, Xiqweek, Vibehole and Richfest, but deliberately
**twice as long and a tenth of the size**. If Vibecamp felt too fast and too big, this is the
other thing.

Third edition. Sold out. 58 people.

Simon's framing: the first was an experiment, *"an initial spell cast. It worked very well ; )"*
The second was the double-down. The third is *"going to be extra charming, as it is the third
time."*

## Why this venue
Simon chose Seegut Blaue Blume for having all four elements: **water** (a lake on the property,
swimmable lakes nearby), **earth** (forest, campground, trees), **fire** (two saunas, fireplaces
in and out, the kitchen), **air** (autumn lake air and a sky dark enough to actually see).

## What actually happens
Pumpkin soup. Bead name bracelets. Woods walks. Massages. Group cooking. Sauna. Wine. A hammock.
Late parties. Spontaneous singing. Philosophical arguments about religion that outlast everyone's
bedtime. Musical circling. Playfighting. A tree planting ceremony. The forest has things in it
that nobody put on a schedule.
