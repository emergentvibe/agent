# Spaces

## The venue
TREEWEEK III is at **Seegut Blaue Blume**, Buchenhain 32, 17268 Boitzenburger Land — a manor
in the Uckermark, about two hours north of Berlin by train and 30 minutes by car.

The house describes its own purpose as *"Es geht uns um nichts Geringeres als die
Wiederverzauberung der Welt"* — "our aim is nothing less than the re-enchantment of the world."
That line is why Simon picked it.

The site sleeps 47 in beds, with around 25 camping spots and 5 RV spots.

## The Office
A room with desks and plugs, for people who have to work during the week. It has a **quiet
policy** — this is the one room where you don't bring the jam. Room-ticket holders can also work
from their own rooms; most have hotel-style desks.

There is a 5G / Starlink line for the event, so there is internet.

## The Art Room
Where art happens. Materials live here.

## The Meditation Room
The multi-purpose room. Meditation and workshops by day; music jamming and partying by night.
If something is happening indoors and it isn't food or work, it is probably happening here.

## The Outside Living Room
A covered outdoor space — sheltered but not indoors. Good for weather that can't decide.

## The Foyer Fireplace
There is a fireplace at the foyer, by the entrance, plus many more fireplaces outside.

## The Bath House
A separate building housing the 12-person dorm and the indoor sauna. **The Bath House is a
female-only space.** The dorm was reduced from 15 beds to 12 so it's less crowded.

## Saunas
There are two.

- **The Bath House sauna** is **electric**, inside the Bath House, and **women only**.
- **The outdoor sauna**, at the back of the house, is **wood-burning**.

Each sauna runs about 4 hours a day. There are 100 sauna towels for the whole event.

## Showers
The Bath House showers are shared with campers, but segregated. Rooms in the main house have
their own en-suite bathrooms. Campers have indoor bathrooms right by the camp site.

## Water and the lakes
There is a lake on the property but **you cannot swim in it — it's full of algae.**

There are other lakes a few steps away that you *can* swim in. Finding them is part of the
week. Ask someone who's been before.

## Stars
The local Bortle score is under 4, which is genuinely dark. On a clear night the sky here is
worth walking out for.

## Forest
There are plenty of trees, forest and outside territory, and a campground with lots of space.
Wander. Things have been known to turn up out there.
