# Food

## How food works
Breakfast is self-serve from 09:00. Lunch is at 13:00 and dinner at 18:00, both cooked by
whoever is on shift that day — which, across the week, is everyone.

The head chef is **Lukas**, Simon's brother. There is a planned menu, so cooking is structured
rather than improvised.

Ingredients are high-quality regional organic produce. Unlimited tea and coffee, and snacks,
all week.

## Vegetarian by default
**Everything the kitchen cooks is vegetarian, and much of it is vegan.** Protein comes mainly
from eggs and legumes.

The one exception is the meat market — see below. If it came out of the kitchen, it's vegetarian.

## Allergens
**Every dish has its allergens listed**, on the printed menu cards and in the day listings below.
All food is vegetarian, so the allergen line also tells you what's vegan: **no milk and no egg
means vegan.**

Two things the allergen list doesn't capture: there is honey in the garlic butter, and the
Kaiserschmarrn compotes.

Everything is prepared in a shared kitchen, so cross-contact is possible. If you have a serious
allergy, talk to the chef rather than relying on a label.

## The meat market
Meat is **not** included in the ticket and does not come out of the kitchen. There is a meat
market on the side: the meat chiefs buy and cook meat separately. Weather and logistics
permitting, there is a BBQ at dinner time for meat eaters.

To log a BBQ purchase, use `/bbq` in the group chat.
Check your tab with `/show_total`. Undo a mistake with `/cancel_purchase`.

## Alcohol
Beer and wine are provided but **not included in the ticket**. Drinks are tracked through the
bot — use `/bar` in the group chat to log what you take.
Check your running tab with `/show_total` and settle up before you leave.

This exists because plenty of people at these events don't drink, and Simon doesn't want
non-drinkers' money paying for other people's wine.
