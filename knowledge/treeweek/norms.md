# How we do things here

## Self-organisation is the point
There is no programme. TREEWEEK is an all-inclusive **self-organised** camp — the workshops,
jams, walks and activities exist because somebody decided to run one. If you're waiting for the
schedule to tell you what to do, you've misread the event.

The stated goal is: *being, together, in a cool location, for a week.*

## You volunteer more than your ticket
From the invitation, more or less verbatim: you volunteer your spirit and your magic powers to
make the event special; your hands, head, joy and tastebuds for shifts in the kitchen and
dishes; your feet, for walking, dancing and carrying yourself with such grace; and your care and
attention, for yourself and others — to notice when someone is in need, and to do something
about it, or to alert someone, or sometimes just to leave them be, because being seen and
witnessed often makes life better on its own.

## Kitchen shifts
Everyone cooks and everyone washes up. Five shift blocks a day; most people do around 1.2 hours
a day, adjusted for how many days they're there.

Nobody works two shifts in one day, and nobody does the same job more than twice all week.
Party-night shifts count double, and the morning after counts one and a half — so taking a hard
shift means doing less across the rest of the week. Work a party-night dish shift and the next
day is yours, guaranteed.

Arrival day is covered by crew and volunteers, because most people are still on a train.

## Cover, don't vanish
Can't make a shift? DM the bot `/cover` and pick your shift — it goes out to the group for
someone to claim. **You are still on it until someone claims it.**

There's no deadline for requesting cover, no scoring, no debt, and nobody tracks who owes what.
Cover someone and you're a legend.

## The alcohol tally
Beer and wine are provided but not included. Log your drinks through the bot using `/bar`.
Check your tab with `/show_total` and settle up before you leave.

## The meat market
The kitchen is vegetarian. Meat runs separately: the meat chiefs buy and cook it. Log BBQ
purchases using `/bbq`.

## The Office is quiet
Some people are working during the week. The Office has a quiet policy. Take the jam elsewhere —
there is a whole multi-purpose room for that.

## The Bath House is female only
The Bath House — the dorm and the indoor sauna — is a women-only space. The showers are shared
with campers but segregated.

## Cleanup
**WHIZBANG is a full-house, all-hands clean** — Friday and Monday at 14:00. Everyone stops and
the house gets sorted together. It is not optional and it is not a small job; it is also over
fast when 58 people do it at once.

There's a smaller mini cleanup on Saturday at midday.

Beyond those: the house is shared, the kitchen is shared, and 58 people leaving one cup each is
58 cups.

## Main character season
The theme for this year, from Simon: go bigger and bolder. Show yourselves, realise your dreams,
LARP an ambitious version of yourself, show your voice, sing your song — actually, at the talent
show.
