# FAQ

## Dates
TREEWEEK III runs **Tuesday 22 September to Tuesday 29 September 2026** — 7 nights, 8 days.
Arrival is Tuesday the 22nd from midday. Departure is early on Tuesday the 29th.

## Getting there
From **Berlin Airport (BER)**: take the FEX towards Berlin Central Station to **Gesundbrunnen**,
then change to the **RE3 towards Stralsund** and ride to **Prenzlau**. The RE3 also departs from
Berlin Central Station.

The journey is about two hours by train, or 30 minutes by car from Prenzlau.

**A van shuttle runs between Prenzlau station and Blaue Blume.** People are batched into full
van trips, so there may be a short wait at the station — travelling with others makes this
better, not worse.

If you are coming by car, drive straight to Seegut Blaue Blume. Drivers are actively welcome —
there are shopping runs and pickups to do.

## Arrival times
All the useful trains into Prenzlau arrive at **:57 past the hour**. The shuttle runs meet them.
Coming in very late makes life hard for whoever is driving — be in Berlin early rather than
landing at 1am.

## Leaving
A **bus leaves Blaue Blume at 10:00 on Tuesday 29 September**, after breakfast, and takes
everyone to Gesundbrunnen. That puts you at the airport in the afternoon. Wake-up is 08:00,
breakfast at 09:00.

## Internet
There is a 5G / Starlink line for the event. It handled everything except video calls well last
year.

## What's included
Seven nights, all food (self-serve breakfast, cooked lunch and dinner), bedding and room towels,
sauna access and sauna towels, unlimited tea and coffee, snacks, and budget for party and art
supplies.

## What costs extra
**Alcohol and meat are not included.** Both work on an honesty tally — see the community norms.

## What to bring
Campers bring their own tent and camping gear. Everyone else: bedding and towels are provided.

## Wifi
The wifi password is **touchgrass**.

## Who to ask
Questions before or during: **TREEWEEK@nowmedia.org**, or Simon and Emergent directly.

## TREECOMPRESSION
Some people stay on in Berlin after the week — this is called TREECOMPRESSION. Most of the team
are in east Berlin (Friedrichshain, Kreuzberg, Neukölln). Plan a longer stay if you want it;
the team are busy just before and tired just after, but dinners happen.
